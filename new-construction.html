Overview

New-construction painting for builders.

New construction painting means painting a house that has never been painted before — everything from the first coat of primer to the last coat of paint, on drywall, trim, doors, cabinets and the outside. Fine Finishes NW is the painting contractor for custom home builders across Southwest Washington, including Axiom Luxury Homes, Soaring Eagle Homes and Jack Harroun Construction. Nine of the ten homes in our portfolio are new builds we painted for those three builders.

Why builders stay with us

Three reasons, every time.

On a custom build, the finish trades can either protect the schedule or destroy it. We know which one we're there to be.

01

We hit the date

We show up when we said, with the crew size the job actually needs, and we finish when we said we would. If something upstream slips, you hear it from us early enough to re-sequence — not on the day the flooring crew arrives.

02

We protect the house

Finished floors, cabinetry, millwork, glass and the trades working around us all get masked and protected. No overspray on someone else's completed work, and no damage claims landing on your desk.

03

The punch list stays short

We walk the house before you do and fix what we find. Your final walkthrough shouldn't be the first time anyone looked closely at our work — and with us, it isn't.

Scope

Everything from primer to punch.

We come in after drywall and carry the finish all the way through to the walkthrough — including the fiddly, slow, high-visibility work that determines how the house photographs and how it shows.

  • Prime & finish drywall — including level-5 smooth walls where the architecture demands it.
  • Trim, doors & millwork — sprayed and finished to a furniture-grade surface.
  • Cabinetry & built-ins — on-site finishing that matches the shop's intent.
  • Cut-in around exposed timber, steel and stone — the slow work, done by hand.
  • Exterior siding, trim, soffits & stain — cedar, fiber cement, board-and-batten.
  • Garage, utility & mechanical spaces — finished, not forgotten.
  • Touch-up & punch — through closing, and back after the movers if you need us.

Builder FAQs

What builders ask us.

How far ahead do you need to be scheduled?

For a full custom home, four to six weeks' notice lets us staff it properly. We'd always rather have a pencilled date early and firm it up later than get a call the week drywall finishes. If you're in a bind, ask anyway — sometimes we can move.

Can you hold a multi-home schedule?

Yes. We've run consecutive homes for the same builder and can staff more than one house at a time. We'll be straight with you about what we can actually cover rather than saying yes and then thinning the crews on both jobs.

Do you carry your own insurance and L&I?

Yes — licensed, bonded and insured in Washington and Oregon, with current certificates and workers' comp available on request. We'll get them to your office before we set foot on the site.

Who handles touch-up after other trades damage the finish?

We do, and we plan for it — some damage from trades following us is a normal part of a build, not a scandal. We'd rather come back and make it right than argue about whose scuff it was.

Will you spec products with our architect or designer?

Happily. We'll sit in on the finish selections, sample on site, and flag early where a specified product or sheen is going to cause problems on a particular substrate — which is a much cheaper conversation to have before the order goes in.

Do you do spec homes and remodels too?

Yes to both. Spec homes, custom builds, whole-house remodels and historic restorations — see the Old Vancouver Restoration for the kind of older-home work we take on.
