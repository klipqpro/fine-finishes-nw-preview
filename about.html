Who we are
“Anyone can apply paint. Few can deliver a finish that still looks flawless a decade later.”

We've spent two decades painting homes and businesses across Southwest Washington and the Portland metro, and in that time we've learned the same lesson over and over: the paint is the easy part.

What separates a finish that lasts from one that fails is everything that happens before the first coat goes on — the washing, the scraping, the filling, the caulking, the priming. It's slow, it's unglamorous, and it's invisible in the final photos. It's also the entire job.

That's why the work in our portfolio is mostly custom homes for builders. Builders can tell the difference. They're on site every day, they've seen what happens when a finish trade cuts corners, and they don't hand a flagship house to someone they're not sure about.

We're a small, locally owned company. When you call, you talk to someone who's actually been on your job. When we quote you, that's the price. And when we say we'll be there Monday, we're there Monday.

What we believe

Four things we won't compromise on.

01

Prep, always

We don't shortcut surface preparation to win a bid. If a job needs three days of prep, the quote has three days of prep in it — and we'll show you why.

02

Premium product

Top-line coatings matched to the surface and our climate, applied at spec thickness. We never thin product to stretch a gallon.

03

Our own people

Trained, background-checked technicians who've been with us for years — not a different set of unknown faces every week.

04

Straight answers

If your ceilings don't need painting, we'll say so. If a color is going to look wrong in your light, we'll tell you before you buy five gallons of it.

05

Licensed & insured

Fully licensed, bonded and insured in both Washington and Oregon. Certificates go to you before we start, not after you ask twice.

06

Written warranty

Every project carries a workmanship warranty in writing, with the terms spelled out up front. If something we did fails, we come back.
