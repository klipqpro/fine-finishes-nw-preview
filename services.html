Overview

Painting services in Southwest Washington.

Fine Finishes NW does five kinds of painting across Southwest Washington and the Portland area: the inside of your house, the outside of your house, kitchen cabinets, new homes for builders, and commercial buildings. Every job starts with prepping the surface properly, and we pick the paint to suit what we're painting and the weather it has to survive. You get the price in writing before we start, and a written warranty when we're done.

01 — Residential

Interior Painting

Walls, ceilings, trim, doors, stairwells and built-ins — prepped properly, cut straight, and finished around your actual life. Your furniture gets moved and covered by us, the site gets tidied every evening, and the house stays livable throughout.

  • Whole-house repaints, single rooms, or trim-only refreshes
  • Low-odor, low-VOC products as standard
  • Free color and sheen consultation, sampled on your walls

Interior painting in detail

02 — Residential

Exterior Painting

Siding, trim, fascia, soffits, doors, decks and fences — washed, scraped, repaired, caulked, primed and coated in the right order. Northwest exteriors fail because of skipped prep, not bad paint, so that's where most of our time goes.

  • Lap, board-and-batten, cedar shingle, fiber cement, stucco, T1-11
  • Rot repair and failed-glazing replacement before coating
  • EPA RRP-safe practice on pre-1978 homes

Exterior painting in detail

03 — For builders

New Construction & Builders

We come in after drywall and carry the finish through to the walkthrough: level-5 walls, sprayed millwork, on-site cabinetry finishing, exterior stain and the slow hand cut-in around exposed timber and steel.

Nearly every home in our portfolio is a custom build we finished for a Northwest builder. They call back because we hit dates, protect the other trades' work, and hand over a short punch list.

Builder services in detail

04 — Specialty

Cabinet Refinishing

A new kitchen for a fraction of the cost of new boxes. Doors and drawer fronts come off and get finished properly; frames are finished in place with the room fully contained so the rest of the house stays clean.

Degreased, deglossed, sanded, primed with a bonding primer and sprayed with a hard cabinet-grade coating — the same process that makes the difference between a finish that survives a family kitchen and one that chips off the drawer edges within a year.

  • Kitchens, bathroom vanities, built-ins and libraries
  • Sprayed finish — no brush marks, no roller stipple
  • Typically 3–5 days, and you keep using the kitchen most of that time

Cabinet refinishing in detail

05 — Commercial

Commercial Painting

Offices, retail, restaurants, medical suites, multi-unit residential and property-management work. Painted on your schedule — which usually means evenings, weekends or phased by floor so your doors stay open.

You get one point of contact, a written scope, and crews who understand that a commercial site has tenants, customers and liability concerns that a house doesn't.

  • After-hours and weekend scheduling at no drama
  • Licensed, bonded and insured — certificates on request
  • Turn-and-burn unit repaints for property managers
