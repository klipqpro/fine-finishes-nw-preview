Overview

Commercial painting in Southwest Washington.

Commercial painting is painting a building people are still using — where the timing matters as much as the paint. Fine Finishes NW paints offices, stores, restaurants, clinics, hallways and apartment buildings in Vancouver, Camas, Ridgefield and the Portland metro. We're licensed, bonded and insured in both Washington and Oregon, and we'll send you the paperwork before we start.

Why it's different

You can't close for a week.

At a house, a paint job is an inconvenience. At your business, every day a space can't be used is money you don't make back. A crew that treats your building like a house will cost you more in lost days than they saved you on the price.

So we work around your hours instead of asking you to work around ours. Nights, weekends, or one floor or one suite at a time while the rest of the building stays open.

You get one person to call who actually answers, a written price that lists what's included, and painters who understand that your building has customers, tenants and deliveries coming and going.

How we work

No surprises, on paper.

Good questions

Commercial work, answered.

What business owners and property managers ask us first.

Can you paint outside our business hours?

Yes, and for most commercial jobs that's how we plan it from the start. Evenings, overnight, weekends, or one floor or suite at a time so the rest of the building keeps running. We'll tell you at the estimate whether the timing you need changes the price, instead of adding a surprise charge later.

Are you licensed and insured for commercial work?

Yes — licensed, bonded and insured in both Washington and Oregon. We send the insurance certificate to you or your property manager before we start, and if your lease says we have to add your company to our policy, we can do that. If a contractor gets vague when you ask this, that tells you something.

Do you repaint apartments between tenants?

Regularly. That work is all about speed and consistency — the same colors and the same standard every time, so units match across a building and an empty one isn't sitting there waiting on us. We can do several units in a row and work to whatever date you need them ready by.

How bad is the smell and the disruption?

Low-smell, low-fume paint is what we use as standard, not an upgrade you pay extra for. In clinics, dental offices and anywhere food is served, we also seal off the area we're working in so the rest of the space isn't affected. That said, no paint is completely odorless while it dries, so in those places we'd usually suggest working after hours anyway.

Do you paint the outside of commercial buildings?

Yes — walls, trim, steel, railings, parking structures and around signs. The same weather rules apply as on houses: it needs to be dry and above about 50°F, so we watch the forecast and don't push a coat on in the wrong conditions. See exterior painting for how we prep surfaces.

Will you work with our contractor or maintenance team?

All the time. Most of our work is for custom home builders, so working alongside other trades and hitting a date is the core of what we do. We'll join your scheduling calls, work to your timeline, and protect the finished work and the other crews around us. See new construction.
